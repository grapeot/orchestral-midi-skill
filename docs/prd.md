# Product Requirements

## Purpose & Audience

Enable musicians and coding agents to transform musical material into explicitly arranged orchestral scores and controlled stereo/headphone hall renders without conflating arrangement quality with acoustic rendering quality.

## Independently Testable Art and Science

- **Art**: Explicit score authoring covering roles, playable ranges, phrase arcs, harmony, repetition and climax pacing, texture thinning, voice leading, real meter changes, note lengths, and true articulations. Program replacement, octave doubling, timing jitter, and wet reverb do not replace score decisions.
- **Science**: Deterministic timing, SF2/FluidSynth or SFZ/sfizz stem rendering with ambience and mixed-library upgrades disclosed, source placement, image-source early reflections, statistical late tail, fixed-head measured HRIR rendering, matched A/B renders, and auditable manifests. External stems can enter independently of Art.

## Boundaries

- Currently a scaffold; implementation lands on a feature branch.
- No automatic role guessing from arbitrary MIDI.
- No bundled third-party assets or invented listening/externalization claims.
